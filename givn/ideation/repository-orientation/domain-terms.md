# Domain terms — repository orientation

Status: all terms below are confirmed, 2026-10-20, by the user after the
Event Storming term check. Collision checks were recorded against the durable
glossary (empty at the time of writing) and the repository (no specs, no
sibling ideation topics). The durable arc42 glossary is proposed at Handoff.
